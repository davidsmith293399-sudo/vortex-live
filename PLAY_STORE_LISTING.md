### 🚀 WHY VORTEXCHAT?

⚡ **CRYSTAL-CLEAR AUDIO AT ~0.6 MB/MIN**
Talk freely for hours without worrying about your monthly cellular bundle. Our fine-tuned Opus voice compression engine operates at ultra-low bitrates (8–12 kbps) with AI background silence suppression, consuming just **~0.6 MB per minute** of voice call time.

Take total control over your video bandwidth. Choose exactly how much data you want to stream:
- **360p Ultra Data Saver**: ~1.15 MB/min (AV1/H.264 baseline, preserves clarity on 2G/3G)
- **480p Eco Saver**: ~2.67 MB/min (Crisp mobile video with 60% bandwidth savings)
- **720p Balanced HD**: ~6.18 MB/min (Smooth group calling for standard LTE or Wi-Fi)
- **1080p High Definition**: ~13.74 MB/min (Studio quality when connected to high-speed broadband)

📊 **REAL-TIME BANDWIDTH TELEMETRY**
No guesswork. VortexChat features a built-in live telemetry HUD that calculates your accumulated session data consumption (in MB) and live transfer rate in real-time. Review your estimated data savings before and during every call.

🖥️ **LOW-BANDWIDTH MOBILE SCREEN SHARING**
Present documents, slides, code, or gameplay without buffering. Select your broadcast frame-rate mode:
- **Eco Slide Share (5 FPS)**: Downsampled for presentations and code review—saves 85% data (~0.68 MB/min)

🛡️ **EXTREME DATA SAVER SWITCH**
Activate with a single tap to instantly lock video feeds to 360p, enable aggressive voice packet compression, and disable unnecessary media previews.

### 🔒 PRIVACY & PERMISSIONS TRANSPARENCY

VortexChat requests only the permissions necessary for peer-to-peer real-time communication:
- **CAMERA**: Required solely when you activate video calling or broadcast your camera stream.
- **RECORD_AUDIO (Microphone)**: Required to transmit your voice during calls.
- **FOREGROUND_SERVICE (Microphone/Camera)**: Enables uninterrupted voice calls while multitasking or when your screen is locked.
- **INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE**: Required to establish WebRTC media connections and monitor cellular/Wi-Fi transitions.

**Zero Data Selling**: We do not sell your personal conversations, messages, or metadata to third parties. All streams utilize secure, end-to-end transport protocols.
